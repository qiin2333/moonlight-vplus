Moonlight V+ is an enhanced version of the popular Moonlight game streaming app, featuring advanced capabilities and optimizations for the ultimate streaming experience.

Pair Moonlight V+ with <a href="https://github.com/AlkaidLab/foundation-sunshine">Foundation Sunshine</a>, our recommended host, to stream games, programs, or your desktop over a local network or the Internet. Standard streaming also works with <a href="https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine">Sunshine</a>. Mouse, keyboard, and controller input is sent from your Android device to the PC.

Streaming performance may vary based on your client device and network setup. HDR requires an HDR10-capable device, a GPU that can encode HEVC Main 10, and an HDR10-enabled game.

'''Enhanced V+ Features'''
* 🚀 **Ultra-High Refresh Rates**: Unlock 144/165Hz streaming for buttery smooth gameplay
* 🎨 **HDR Support**: Complete HDR content streaming with automatic device calibration
* 📱 **Custom Resolutions**: Support for custom resolutions, aspect ratios, and asymmetric resolutions
* 🎮 **Advanced Touch Controls**: Enhanced multi-touch, stylus support, and touchpad mode
* 🎯 **Gyro Assistant**: Built-in gyroscope support for motion controls and aiming
* 📊 **Performance Monitoring**: Real-time FPS, bitrate, latency, and packet loss display
* 🎛️ **Scene Presets**: Save and switch between different streaming configurations
* 🎤 **Microphone Redirection**: Remote voice chat with Foundation Sunshine 2025.0720 or later
* 🖥️ **Desktop Enhancement**: Beautiful app thumbnails and custom sorting
* 🔧 **Smart Updates**: Automatic update checking with intelligent notifications

'''Core Features'''
* Open-source and completely free (no ads, IAPs, or "Pro")
* Streams games purchased from any store
* Works on your home network or over the Internet/LTE
* Up to 4K 120 FPS HDR streaming with 7.1 surround sound
* H.264, HEVC, and AV1 codec support (AV1 requires Sunshine and a supported host GPU)
* Keyboard and mouse support (best with Android 8.0 or later)
* Stylus/S-Pen support
* Supports PS3/4/5, Xbox 360/One/Series, and Android gamepads
* Local co-op with up to 16 connected controllers (max of 4 controllers with GFE)
* Force feedback and gamepad motion sensor support
* Mouse control via gamepad by long-pressing Start

'''Quick Setup with Foundation Sunshine'''
# Install Foundation Sunshine on your PC from https://github.com/AlkaidLab/foundation-sunshine/releases
# Complete first-time setup in its web interface
# Select the PC in Moonlight V+ and enter the displayed PIN in the host web interface
# Start streaming!

Foundation Sunshine is recommended for the full V+ experience, including supported extensions such as microphone redirection. Standard Sunshine remains available for standard streaming; older NVIDIA GameStream setups may still work.

To have a good experience, you need a mid to high-end wireless router with a good wireless connection to your Android device (5 GHz highly recommended) and a good connection from your PC to your router (Ethernet highly recommended).
